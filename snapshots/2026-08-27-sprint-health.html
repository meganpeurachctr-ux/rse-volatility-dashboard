TRI-55877 was created 5 minutes 26 seconds before the sprint started and resolved at 14:05:33 PDT — 32 minutes after creation. Under David’s framework it is “pre-sprint committed scope.” In practice it is a ticket written and closed on the same afternoon.

This is not a data error; it is a limitation of the framework the metric is built on. When a sprint starts at 13:38 on a working day, “created before sprint start” includes everything written that morning, and the pre-sprint bucket absorbs what is functionally a planning dump. Sprint 7630 had the same shape (start 13:44 PDT) but a smaller effect. Here it is 20.9% of story points.

Two consequences for retro
  1. A clean 0% injection rate on Day 2 is partly an artifact of timing. Had sprint 7644 started at 09:00 on 2026-08-26, roughly 14 of these 17 items (32.0 SP) would be day-1 dump instead of pre-sprint — the sprint would open with a ~15% day-1 bucket rather than a 0% one.
  2. The day-1 dump metric is measuring the clock as much as the behaviour. If the intent is “work that appeared after planning finished,” the boundary should be the planning meeting, not the sprint-start button. Worth a five-minute conversation with David.
