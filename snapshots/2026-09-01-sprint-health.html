Assessment

The biggest risk is that 45 items and 87.0 SP — 45.8% of the sprint's points — have not been started on Day 7 of 15, while scope is still growing. The team closed 32.0 SP in the four days since 2026-08-28, a real acceleration, but sustaining the sprint needs roughly 20 SP per remaining day against an observed best of 15.0 SP in a day. Something will carry over; the useful question now is what, chosen deliberately rather than discovered on 2026-09-09.

The second risk is measurement, not delivery. Contamination tagging fell to 40% on true injections, 12 of 32 Done items have no resolution or resolution date (silently removing 18.0 SP from every date-keyed metric), and this run turned up the first provable case of an injection whose created date predates its sprint-entry date by several days. None of these change what shipped; all three change what the retro will be able to see.

The clear positive is Chris Fajardo's CDK chain: 10 of 11 assigned items and 12 of 13 SP Done, including a five-ticket unplanned migration executed inside three days. It is the single largest completion block in the sprint — and also the largest untagged block. Backfilling those four Contamination Source values is the cheapest available improvement to this sprint's data quality.
