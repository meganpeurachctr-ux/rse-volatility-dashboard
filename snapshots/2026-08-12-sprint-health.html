Assessment

RSE 26Q3 S3 7/29-8/12 reaches close (2026-08-12, 20:55:41Z UTC) with 106 items / 191.5 SP in scope and 90 items / 162 SP done (84.9% / 84.6%), 15.1 points short of a full burn-down and 8.9 points behind the prior sprint's 93.8% final completion. The late recovery was real: Days 14 and 15 closed 35 items and 65 SP between them, and carryover landed at 16 items / 29.5 SP against the 70–75 SP projected on 2026-08-10 and 2026-08-11.

The single biggest risk is now one person, not two. Pooja Kabra moved off zero on close day (TRI-54835, 3 SP, closed 09:49 PDT with a substantive results write-up), and her three remaining items were all actively edited this morning. That leaves Carla Bellido as the only assignee finishing with zero completions — 4 items / 8 SP, 27.1% of all remaining SP — and her queue is not just incomplete, it is inactive: last touches are 2026-07-29, 2026-08-04, 2026-08-06 and 2026-08-11, with nothing on close day. Krish Sharma's 4 open items / 6.5 SP are similarly stale (latest 2026-08-06). Ten of the 16 carryover items have not been touched since 2026-08-06 or earlier, which is the number to bring to retro rather than the headline carryover figure.

The finding that would otherwise have been missed is the close-day rescoping — five items retitled in place with no change to created date or story points, so injection rate, scope composition and the estimate-change counter all register zero. See the rescope table above.

On the positive side: 13 of 19 assignees finished at 100% (up from 10 in Revision 1), contamination tagging reached 100% on true injections for the first time this sprint, all four close-day resolutions were genuine Done outcomes with no churn, and the volatility dashboard is publishing current data again. Contamination tagging is not a process gap.

Method caveats. (1) The Jira MCP ignores field-level projection and returns full issue bodies, so a 106-issue detail pull exceeds response limits; totals were derived from reconciled count-mode aggregates (items 106, SP 191.5, done 90, SP done 162, all cross-checking to the assignee table) with the 16 open items pulled in full detail. (2) Revision 1 reported 86 items done because its Jira reads were taken near the start of the run while the file was written at 10:14 PDT — four items resolved at 07:50, 08:26, 09:09 and 09:49 PDT were missed. Daily snapshots should carry an explicit "data as of HH:MM" stamp separate from the file date so this gap is visible without a re-read.
