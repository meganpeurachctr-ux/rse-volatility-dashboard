Assessment

RSE 26Q3 S3 7/29-8/12 reaches its close day (2026-08-12, sprint ends 20:55:41Z UTC) with 106 items / 191.5 SP in scope and 86 items / 152 SP done (81.1% / 79.4%), 18.9 points short of a full burn-down.

The single biggest remaining risk is concentrated carryover, not carryover volume. Of the 20 items / 39.5 SP still open, 8 items / 17 SP belong to two people who finished the sprint with zero completions — Pooja Kabra (4 items / 9 SP, two still in To Do) and Carla Bellido (4 items / 8 SP, one still Open). Two assignees holding 40% of the carryover is a capacity or blocking problem that a bulk sprint-rollover will hide, and it is the same pair flagged on 2026-08-11. Deciding before close whether their work is re-estimated or reassigned matters more than the aggregate number.

On the positive side, the finish was genuinely strong: Days 14 and 15 closed 31 items and 55 SP between them, 10 of 19 assignees ended at 100% completion, total carryover landed at 39.5 SP (20.6%) against the 70–75 SP projected two runs ago, and contamination tagging reached 100% on true injections for the first time this sprint. Contamination tagging is not a process gap — the one remaining untagged item is a day-1 dump story outside the nudge framework, and the new "Planning Absence" value should be added to the tag glossary.

Method caveat: the Jira MCP ignores field-level projection and returns full issue bodies, so a 106-issue detail pull exceeds response limits.
