Assessment

Completion pace remains the biggest risk, and the arithmetic has gotten harder even though the trend improved. At Day 9 of 15 the sprint is 42.2% done by item and 36.0% by story point against a 60.0% linear ideal — gaps of 17.8 and 24.0 points, both narrower than the 21.5 and 26.5 recorded on 2026-08-19. Two consecutive strong days (14 items / 20.5 SP closed in the last interval, none of it arriving pre-closed) is the best throughput evidence this sprint has produced. But 130.5 SP remain against six days, requiring 21.8 SP/day to close clean. The observed average is 8.2 SP/day and the prior sprint (RSE 26Q3 S3) managed roughly 12.2 SP/day across its full run. Projecting at those two rates lands the sprint between 60% and 72% complete, or 57 to 82 SP of carryover, against the baseline sprint's 20 SP (9%). Two good days have not changed the destination; they have only made the required daily rate less absurd.

The carryover risk is concentrated, not spread — and that is actionable. Six assignees hold 28 items / 52.0 SP and have completed 5.8% of it. That cluster is roughly a quarter of sprint scope and accounts for the bulk of the projected shortfall, while four other people sit at 71–100% done. This is the most useful new finding in this snapshot: the sprint does not have a uniform pace problem, it has six stalled queues.

Volatility crossed its second threshold today. SP-weighted injection reached 10.5%, joining item-level injection (12.9%) outside the ~10% guideline; both are now breached for the first time this sprint. Absolute injection is still under the baseline's final 21%/16%, so the verdict holds at moderately volatile, but 87% of all injection SP arrived in the 2026-08-17 → 2026-08-19 window and the run-rate points at ~20% by close. Composition is the more useful read: Dependency Unblock at 5 of 15 is decisively the leading reason, well ahead of Stakeholder Urgent (2). The story for retro is blocked-work handoffs and upstream readiness, not stakeholder interruption.

Two clear positives. Contamination tagging on true injections hit 100% (15/15), up from 83.3%; both outstanding backfills were closed by the assignee who received automated nudges, and all three injections created on 2026-08-19 arrived pre-tagged. The nudge loop is working. All-additions coverage also cleared 80% for the first time at 81.0%. And estimation discipline is fully intact nine days in — 116/116 estimated, nothing above 3 SP. Contamination tagging is no longer a process gap on this sprint.
