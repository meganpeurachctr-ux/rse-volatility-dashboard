Assessment

Sprint 26Q3 S5 reaches its end date at 77.9% of items and 72.1% of SP complete, against sprint 6801’s 89% / 91%.

The single biggest risk is no longer schedule — it is carryover: 27 items and 60 SP unfinished, 28% of the sprint against a baseline of 9%, with 20 of those items mid-flight rather than untouched. That volume does not absorb into S6 without displacing what was planned for it, and prior sprints show unfinished items often fail to move to the successor roster at all — worth a deliberate check at close rather than an assumption.

Two structural findings persist. The descope channel removed 13 SP of never-started work in the final five days, inflating the headline by roughly four points on each axis. Contamination tagging on a sprint-entry basis sits at 74.3%, below threshold for the fourth consecutive run, because the six untagged items were all created before the sprint and are structurally unreachable by a nudge rule keyed on created.

The genuine positive: the close-day surge is real work this time — 28 of 30 newly-Done items are clean transitions, with zero same-day create-and-close and zero retroactive already-Done imports, a direct improvement on the 2026-09-04 finding.

Resolution hygiene remains the quiet one to fix: 13 Done items (20 SP) carry no resolution date, 11 from a single assignee, and every one is invisible to velocity, burndown and the dashboard.
