Recommendation for the next sprint: the dominant driver here is not injection volume, it is injection cause — Mis-Planning (10) and Planning Absence (3) together account for 13 of 25 injections, meaning more than half of all mid-sprint scope traces back to sprint planning rather than to genuine discovery. Nothing in the volatility score penalises that, because a correctly-tagged Mis-Planning item scores identically to a correctly-tagged Production Incident. Recommend splitting the untagged-share component into a planning-attributable share so the leaderboard distinguishes scope that could have been foreseen from scope that could not, and separately instrument the move-out channel — nine items leaving before close is the same order of magnitude as the nine that carried, and only one of the two is currently measured.

Methodology

How the volatility score is computed

Five components, each a 0–1 ratio, weighted and renormalised so the active weights sum to 100. Density denominators are max(owned items, 1).

Bands: Clean <10 · Healthy 10–25 · Watch 25–45 · Volatile 45–70 · Critical ≥70.

Known limits of this snapshot
  • Churn is unmeasured. In-place rescopes and re-estimates register on no component; its 15 points redistribute across the other four.
  • Move-outs are unscored. Nine items (22 SP) left before close and carry only the successor sprint's reference. Shown above, excluded from every score.
  • Attribution is close-time, not event-time. Without changelog wiring, injections are attributed to the current assignee, so close and event attribution are identical by construction.
  • created is not sprint-entry date. An item written weeks earlier and pulled in mid-sprint is filed as pre-sprint scope and never counts as an injection.
  • 14 Done items carry no resolution date. They are treated as Done-at-close; if any actually closed after the close instant, carry-over here is understated.
  • Small denominators distort. RSEs with one or two owned items produce coarse ratios; read the band with the item count next to it.
