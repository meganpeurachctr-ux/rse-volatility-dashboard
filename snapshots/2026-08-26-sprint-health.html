Assessment

The sprint landed, and it landed well — 93.1% of items and 92.1% of SP done, ahead of the RSE 26Q2 S1 4/8-4/22 baseline's 89% / 91% on both axes. Carryover is 8 items / 16.0 SP (7.9% of SP), slightly better than the baseline's 20 SP (9%). Injection finished at 18.1% of items and 16.2% of SP — below baseline on items, level on SP — and every one of the 21 true mid-sprint injections was completed. Zero story-point re-estimates occurred across all fifteen days. On the numbers leadership will see, this is a good sprint.

The biggest risk is that yesterday's snapshot called this sprint a failure and today's calls it a success, and the difference is one day of status transitions that no metric can validate. 31 items / 63.0 SP moved to Done in the final 24 hours, against a sprint-long best of 29.0 SP over three days. Of that 63.0 SP, 11.0 SP (17.5%) is traceable to bookkeeping rather than observable delivery: 5.0 SP of same-day retroactive injections closed within minutes of creation, and 6.0 SP of items renamed and closed in the same interval — including TRI-54779, which went from "Deploy to vehicle" to "Athena 2 Linux Box Validation & Testing" on the day it was marked done. The count of Done-with-null-resolutiondate items doubled to ten. Adjusted, the surge is ~52 SP, still a real and impressive push — but the specific retro question is whether Day 15 finished work or closed tickets, and the answer is not in Jira.

Contamination tagging regressed below threshold and the descope channel stayed open to the last hour. Coverage on all additions fell to 76.9% (from 83.3%), driven by two close-day injections arriving untagged; both were nudged. Coverage on true injections fell from a hollow 100% to 90.5% — confirming the 100% was an artifact of removal, exactly as flagged on 2026-08-25. Two more items left the sprint unfinished today, bringing cumulative undocumented scope movement to 13 items; only one of the two (TRI-47662) went somewhere nameable. On the whole-sprint counterfactual, completion is 83.7% of everything that was ever in scope, versus the 93.1% Jira will report.

Three concrete retro asks, unchanged in substance from 2026-08-25 and now better evidenced: (1) require a reason code when an item is pulled out of an active sprint, the same way Contamination Source is required when one is pushed in — 13 undocumented removals in one sprint is the largest measurement gap in this program; (2) treat a summary change on an item already in an active sprint as a scope event requiring a note, because a renamed-then-closed ticket earns credit for work that was never committed; (3) close the tagging loop before close day rather than after, since nudging a Done item is a formality. The one positive worth carrying forward without qualification: injected work is getting finished at 100%, and re-estimation churn was zero all sprint.
