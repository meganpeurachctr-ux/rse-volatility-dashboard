Methodology

How the volatility score is computed

Five components, each a 0–1 ratio, weighted and renormalised so the active weights sum to 100. Density denominators are max(owned items, 1).

Known limits of this snapshot
  • Churn is unmeasured. In-place rescopes and re-estimates register on no component; its 15 points redistribute across the other four.
  • Move-outs are unscored. 16 items (33 SP) left before close. Shown above, excluded from every score.
  • Orphaned carry-over. All 8 carry-over items stayed in the closed sprint; 0 moved to RSE 26Q3 S7 9/23-10/7.
  • Attribution is close-time, not event-time. Without changelog wiring, injections are attributed to the current assignee, so close and event attribution are identical by construction.
  • created is not sprint-entry date. An item written earlier and pulled in mid-sprint is filed as pre-sprint scope and never counts as an injection.
  • 4 Done items carry no resolution date (TRI-55868, TRI-56281, TRI-56590, TRI-56616). They are treated as Done-at-close.
  • 3 items closed Not Needed / Won't Do count as Done (status category done), so they lower their owners' carry-over.
  • Sub-tasks excluded. 13 Sub-task issues are not in the scored roster.
  • Small denominators distort. RSEs with one or two owned items produce coarse ratios; read the band with the item count next to it.
