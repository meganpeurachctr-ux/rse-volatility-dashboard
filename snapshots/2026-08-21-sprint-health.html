Composition is unchanged: Dependency Unblock leads at 5 of 15, Stakeholder Urgent is only 2. For retro, the unplanned-work story is blocked-work handoffs and upstream readiness. Injected work is also being absorbed well — 8 of 15 injections Done (53% by SP) versus the sprint's own 40.2% — suggesting these interrupts are small and tractable rather than destabilising.
