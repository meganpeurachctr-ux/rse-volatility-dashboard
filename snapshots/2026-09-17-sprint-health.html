Assessment

The single most important number today is that SP remaining did not move: 160.5 yesterday, 160.5 today. Scope grew 15 SP and completion grew 15 SP. On a percentage view the sprint gained four points; on the work-left view it stood perfectly still. Any read of S6 that quotes only the completion percentage overstates progress.

Two-thirds of the day’s apparent delivery was bookkeeping. Of 15 SP marked Done, only 5 SP came from genuine transitions on work already in the sprint; 10 SP walked in already finished, 8 of it in a four-ticket burst created and closed inside 117 seconds on 2026-09-16 at 19:32. Across the sprint, 13 of 97 Done SP (13.4%) were resolved within ten minutes of creation. The work is presumably real; the board is partly being used as an after-the-fact ledger, and no metric separates the two.

Delivery is concentrated and the required run-rate is out of reach on the demonstrated average. John Shepherd holds 40.2% of all Done SP. S6 needs 35.7 SP/day across ~4.5 effective working days against a sprint-to-date 17.8. Projections span 69–90% of SP; hitting S5’s 90.7% requires sustaining the best two days of the sprint every remaining day. Roughly 60–80 SP needs a destination, and Day 9 is the right time to decide that.

Three real positives. Honest completion (arrivals excluded) rose 33.8% → 35.9% SP, so genuine motion did occur. Not-started SP fell 26.4% → 22.1% for a fourth consecutive day, with in-flight SP at 40.2%. And contamination tagging on true injections held at 16/16 = 100% for a third straight day.

One process defect worth fixing. The tagging metric reads 100% only because it classifies by created. On a sprint-entry basis it is 79.2%, below threshold, and items like TRI-55418 are structurally un-nudgeable. Re-keying step 3b on sprint-entry date would close it.
