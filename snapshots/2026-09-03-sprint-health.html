Assessment

The sprint will not land on its current commitment.132.0 SP remain with six days to run, requiring 22.0 SP/day against a nine-day average of 8.1 and a best-ever day of 15.0. Two of the six remaining days are a weekend. 24 items carrying 52.0 SP have not been started and 42 items / 80.0 SP are in flight, so the shortfall is not a matter of finishing what is nearly done — roughly a quarter of the sprint’s points have not been touched on Day 9 of 15. The realistic decision is which 40–60 SP to carry or cut deliberately, in the next day or two, rather than discovering it on 2026-09-09.

Descoping has already started, informally. Four unstarted items were removed in the last 25 hours — two of them roughly two minutes before this read — and one, TRI-55932, was a Stakeholder Urgent item injected on Day 6 and abandoned on Day 9 without being started. This is the descope channel this report has flagged as unmeasured for weeks, now showing up live: it registers on no metric, it silently improves completion by 1.4 pts of items and 1.3 pts of SP, and once that item left the roster the only surviving record of a stakeholder interrupt this sprint is this document. Read against the 2026-09-02 roster, the sprint moved backwards today (42.9% → 42.1% items) despite five items closing.

Throughput halved and one of today’s closes was bookkeeping. +7.0 SP against +12.0 the day before, and the fifth of five closes was TRI-55569 arriving already Done with no estimate — an item in the numerator representing no work done inside this sprint and no points in either SP figure. It is also the first break in this sprint’s 100% estimation coverage.

Unplanned work passed the baseline on a points basis. By sprint entry, 22 items / 37.0 SP — 19.3% of items and 18.0% of SP — were not in the plan, against baseline sprint 6801’s 16% SP finish. Reaching that on Day 9 of 15 means this sprint is on track to be materially more contaminated than the sprint that prompted this analysis. The created-date view understates it at 13.7%; the gap between the two views is the growing set of pre-sprint tickets admitted mid-flight.

Two positives.Contamination tagging held at 88.2%, above the 80% threshold, and both gaps are explained by the admitted-late mechanism rather than neglect — TRI-56067 was created and tagged the same day, so creation-time tagging is real for items born inside the sprint. And in-place rescoping stopped: zero renames and zero re-estimates in the last 25 hours, after three of each the day before. Yesterday’s silent-rescope burst was an episode, not a trend.

One process recommendation. Descope is now the third scope channel to show up unmeasured, after in-place renames and in-place re-estimates. All three are visible only because this report diffs the full roster between runs. The roster diff — not any single tile — should be the primary instrument, and the dashboard needs a descope count before the next sprint starts.
