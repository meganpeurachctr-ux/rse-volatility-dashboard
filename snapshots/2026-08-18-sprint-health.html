Assessment

The biggest risk is completion pace, not volatility. At Day 7 of 15 the sprint is 21.5% done by item and 17.6% done by story point against a 46.7% linear ideal — roughly 25 to 29 points behind, and the SP gap is wider than the item gap, meaning the larger stories are the ones not moving. Some of that is status lag rather than real lag: 40 items (37.4% of scope) sit In Progress, and if even half land this week the picture changes quickly. But 44 items are still untouched in To Do with eight working days left, and the sprint has absorbed 13 SP of unplanned work on top of that.

The second concern is the 2026-08-17 spike. Five items in one day is not the steady trickle the first five days suggested, and two are tagged Mis-Planning — the team saying, in its own words, that this work should have been caught at planning. That is the actionable signal here: a refinement-quality problem, not an interruption problem, and fixable before the next sprint starts.

One genuine positive. Contamination tagging on true injections is at 87.5% with a single backfill outstanding, and that one has been nudged. Estimation coverage remains 100% with no story above 3 SP, so sizing is consistent. Overall injection at 7.5% items / 6.7% SP remains well under the baseline's 21% / 16%. The sprint is not out of control — it is behind, with a refinement gap that surfaced on one identifiable day.
