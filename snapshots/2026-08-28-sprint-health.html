At 11.5% of items and 9.0% of SP against a 20.0% Day-3 ideal, this sprint is 8.5 and 11.0 points behind, and 58 of 96 items — 113.0 SP, 63.5% of the sprint’s points — have not been started. Twenty status transitions in 24 hours say the board is genuinely being worked, so this reads as a slow start rather than a stall, but the gap needs to close in the next three working days; S4 reached 96.6% only by way of a heavy final week.

The positive signal is real: contamination tagging is at 100% and the single true injection was tagged at creation, before any nudge could fire. After a run of sprints where coverage had to be dragged up by automated nudges — and after the 2026-08-26 nudge produced a confirmed backfill on TRI-55838 — a mid-sprint ticket arriving already labelled suggests the habit is starting to take. One data point is one data point, but it is the right one.

Contamination tagging is not a process gap this run. Coverage is 100% on both additions and true injections, no nudges were required, and no backfill candidates exist. The adjacent hygiene problem is resolution: two items reached Done with a null resolution and null resolution date, which quietly corrupts every velocity and churn figure computed from resolution dates.

Two measurement caveats carry forward. First, the empty day-1 dump is a clock artifact as much as an achievement — use the combined day-0-plus-day-1 figure of 17 items / 37.0 SP when comparing to S1–S4. Second, rename detection failed this run: the 2026-08-27 HTML twin stored paraphrased, truncated summaries in SNAPSHOT.roster, so 65 of 95 items appeared renamed when none were. This twin stores verbatim summaries, restoring in-place-rescope detection from the 2026-08-29 run onward.
