Assessment

The biggest risk is carryover, and it is effectively locked in. With 51 items / 97.0 SP incomplete and two working days left, this sprint closes near 60–65% SP done and hands roughly 75–80 SP to the next sprint. That is large enough that RSE 26Q3 S5's planning capacity should be treated as already largely consumed. The decision worth making before 2026-08-26 is which of the 41 in-flight items get finished versus consciously pushed — rather than letting the sprint boundary make that call by default.

Second risk: volatility is no longer the story, and reporting may still be treating it as one. Three consecutive snapshots have led with volatility narrative while the completion gap widened from 19.7 to 29.2 points. This snapshot's predecessor called the burst "bounded" two days before injection resumed — two data points are not a trend in either direction.

The clear positive is throughput. 14 items / 29.0 SP across an interval that included a full weekend is the best sustained delivery of the sprint, with 41 items in flight and 13 already in review. The team is not stalled. Contamination tagging on true injections is healthy at 94.7%, with the one gap created and nudged the same day — the July process gap is closed.

Retro note: invisible scope movement is the most under-measured thing on this board — six items across three runs, four backdated pull-ins and two silent descopes, none of it registering on any injection or completion metric. The roster embed should be treated as permanent.
