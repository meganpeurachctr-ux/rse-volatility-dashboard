Assessment

The biggest risk is that the burn-rate gap is now unclosable by delivery alone. 123.0 SP remain across 5 days, requiring 24.6 SP/day; the best day of this sprint was 16.0 and the ten-day average is 9.6. Sprint 7644 will not finish its current scope, and the arithmetic has said so since Day 7. What changed today is how the number is being made to look better: four items completed before the sprint began were imported into the roster already Done, adding 6.0 SP to both sides of the ratio at a 100% completion rate. That is the fourth distinct channel — after injection, descope, and unestimated arrival — by which this sprint’s completion percentage has moved without anyone finishing more work, and the only one that lands in the pre-sprint bucket, where the classifier reads it as healthy planning.

The positive is real. Excluding every artifact, 2026-09-03 delivered 17.0 SP — the strongest day of the sprint by a clear margin — and 12 items moved forward a status. Estimation coverage returned to 100%%, and yesterday’s tagging nudge was honoured within a day, making it seven backfills out of eight nudges. The team is executing better than the headline percentage suggests; the reporting around it is what keeps degrading.

Two process gaps for the retro. First, contamination tagging by sprint entry is 76.7% — below the 80% threshold — and the five untagged items are all late entrants the nudge rule structurally cannot reach, because it keys on created rather than sprint-entry date. Second, 12 of 13 undated Done items belong to one person, which makes every date-keyed metric here understate that individual’s throughput and overstate everyone else’s share.
