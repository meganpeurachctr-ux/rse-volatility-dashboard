Assessment

The sprint will not land, and the numbers are now flattering it. With one working day left before close on 2026-08-26, 41 items / 82.0 SP remain open — 40.7% of scope. Completion sits 28.7 points (items) and 34.0 points (SP) behind the linear ideal, and the SP gap widened day-over-day even as the headline percentage rose seven points. Expect roughly 75–80 SP of carryover, against the baseline sprint's 20 SP (9%).

The biggest risk is no longer injection — it is undocumented descoping. Five items, three of them In Progress, left the sprint on Day 14 with nothing recorded about why. Eleven such movements have accumulated across four runs, none visible to any existing metric or to the volatility dashboard, all detected only by diffing the roster embed.

The concrete retro ask is process, not tooling: require a reason code when an item is pulled out of an active sprint, the same way Contamination Source is required when one is pushed in. Until then the descope channel stays unmeasured, and it is currently doing more damage to sprint predictability than injection is.
