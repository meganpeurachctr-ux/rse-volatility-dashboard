Assessment. S5 finished at 93.8% of items and 90.7% of story points as reported, and at 86.9% / 81.5% on the honest denominator — the gap is nine unfinished items moved out of the sprint in the last eight hours before close. That is a new mechanism: previous sprints lost items to descopes that went nowhere, whereas these nine all landed in S6. No work was abandoned, but the completion rate was flattered by 6.9 points on items and 9.2 on story points purely through the order of operations at close.

Unfinished work is the real problem: 40 SP against a 20 SP baseline — twice the rate, and the second consecutive sprint above it. Three of those items are orphaned in no sprint at all, including one that had reached In Review.

The positives are real. Contamination tagging finished at 92.0%, the second-best in the series. All three long-dormant zombie issues closed. The close window decomposes to eleven genuine transitions with zero same-day create-and-close, zero renames and zero retroactive imports. Estimation coverage was 100%.

For the retro, the structural question is planning quality, not interruption. Eighteen of the 23 tagged injections were Mis-Planning or Planning Absence; only three were production incidents. The team is not being interrupted from outside so much as discovering its own scope late.
